# Updated (Final) Design Summary

Since the initial concept design presented in week 2, we have made a couple of important changes to better accommodate our app. The original concepts were UserAuthentication, Sessioning, Review, Playlist, and Friending. Since then, we have added the MusicDiscovery, Profile, Requesting, and Upvote concepts. While developing Noted, we included these concepts to maintain relevant information. Song information is encapsulated within the MusicDiscovery concept, supplementary information about a user (bio, profile picture, etc.) is maintained in the Profile concept, and liking/unliking is maintained in the Upvote concept. We also utilized the Requesting concept to implement necessary syncs within the app, including authorization and initialization.

We originally created the Playlist concept to allow users to create their own playlists, similar to apps such as GoodReads. However, we felt that this feature would introduce friction since users could create these playlists directly in apps such as Spotify or Apple Music. Therefore, we decided on internally creating playlists for "Listen Later", "Favorites", and "Friend Recommendations" upon user registration instead. We decided to create the Friend Recommendation playlist after receiving feedback from the peer review. Our peers discussed the possibility of directly recommending music to their friends, so the Friend Recommendation feature aims to solve this problem.

In terms of the UI, we remained pretty faithful to the original sketches and visual design study. We added more vibrant colors and icons that integrated smoothly into the original design.
